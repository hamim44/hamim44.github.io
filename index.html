Frequently asked questions

Does Toolumi need an internet connection?

No. Every tool works offline. The only features that use the network are ads on the Free plan, anonymous usage statistics and purchases through Apple.

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings in the app and choose Upgrade to Pro. Toolbox Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime unlock; current prices are shown in the app in your local currency. Pro also unlocks larger batches and higher limits in the file tools.

I bought Pro but the app still shows ads

Open Settings → Upgrade to Pro and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. If it still does not unlock, email us with the date of purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: on your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Toolbox Pro and cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for in-app purchases are decided by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my files?

Results are saved where you choose: the Files app, your photo library or the app’s own storage. Your files are never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why did I see a consent screen about ads?

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions, Google’s consent form appears before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy options. Toolumi only ever requests non-personalized ads and never asks for tracking permission.

Something is wrong with a tool

Email us the tool name, what you did and what happened. If a file caused the problem, tell us its type and approximate size; please do not send private documents.
