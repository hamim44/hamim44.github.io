1. Summary

  • Every tool in Toolumi runs on your device. Your files, photos and text are never uploaded to us. We have no servers and no user accounts.
  • The only components that talk to the internet are Google’s advertising SDK (Free plan only), Google Analytics for Firebase (anonymous usage statistics) and Apple’s in-app purchase system. None of them receives your content.
  • Free users see ads from Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only and never ask for permission to track you.
  • Anonymous usage statistics tell us which tools are used and for how long, so we know what to improve. They never contain what you type, open or create.
  • Toolbox Pro removes ads. Payments are handled entirely by Apple.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with anyone beyond what is described here.

2. Who we are

Toolumi is an independent app developed by Abrar Hamim. Questions about this policy can be sent to abrarhamim4044j@gmail.com.

3. What the app does with your content

Toolumi is a collection of utilities (text, calculation, unit conversion, photo and PDF tools, finance, generators, health and more). Everything you type, pick or scan is processed locally on your iPhone:

  • Files you open stay on your device. Results are saved only where you choose: the app’s own storage, the Files app, or your photo library.
  • Sharing is always something you start yourself through the iOS share sheet.
  • Settings such as language, appearance, favourites and tool preferences are stored on the device only.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

The Diagnostics screen in Settings shows counters (for example how many ads were shown) that are kept on your device for your own inspection. We do not use a crash-reporting service.

5. Advertising (Free plan)

If you use Toolumi without Toolbox Pro, the app shows ads delivered by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). This is the only third-party service that receives any data from the app, and it does so under Google’s own privacy terms.

  • Non-personalized ads. Every ad request asks Google for non-personalized ads, which are chosen from context rather than from a profile of you.
  • No tracking permission. Toolumi never shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the ad SDK.
  • What Google may collect. To serve and measure ads, Google may receive information such as your IP address, device and app information, a device identifier, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google describes this in its Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can manage Google’s ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform appears before any ad is requested. Where this applies you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Ad attribution. Apple’s SKAdNetwork framework lets advertisers measure app installs in a privacy-preserving way without identifying you. The ad networks involved are listed in the app’s configuration, as Apple requires.
  • Pro users. When Toolbox Pro is active (including the free trial and the lifetime unlock), the advertising SDK is never started.

6. Usage statistics

To learn which tools people actually use, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service Google provides to us.

  • What is sent. Short events with fixed names: a tool was opened or finished, and which tool is on screen and for how long, or that the list of tools is on screen (always by the tool’s English name and the name of its category, for example “Prime Number, Education & Math”); a Free-plan limit was reached; the upgrade screen was shown; a purchase was completed, cancelled or restored (with the product ID); an ad was shown. With them Google receives technical information such as the app version, device model, operating system version, language, an app-instance identifier created by the app, and the country or region it derives from the IP address. Two coarse properties are attached: whether the plan is Free or Pro, and the app language.
  • What is never sent. Nothing you type, no file, photo or PDF, no file name, no result of a calculation, no account or contact detail. The app sets no user ID, does not use the advertising identifier and never asks for permission to track you.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland nothing is collected until you have agreed in the consent form to storing information on your device. You can change that answer at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options, and collection stops at once when you withdraw it. If the form was never shown to you there, for example because you use Toolbox Pro, usage statistics stay off.
  • Elsewhere. In other regions the statistics are collected for all users, Free and Pro, as described here; the app has no separate setting for them.
  • Not used for advertising. The statistics are not used to personalise ads; the app tells Google Analytics that advertising storage and ad personalisation are not allowed.
  • Retention. Google keeps event data for the limited retention period set in Google Analytics and processes it under its own terms: Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. Purchases

Toolbox Pro is sold as auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time lifetime unlock through Apple’s in-app purchase system.

  • Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, address or payment details.
  • The app verifies your purchase on the device with Apple’s StoreKit and keeps only the information needed to unlock Pro, such as which product you bought and when a subscription renews or expires. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in the iOS Settings app under your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Offer codes are redeemed through Apple’s own redemption sheet.

8. Data sharing and retention

We never receive your content, and we do not sell data. The only parties that process data in connection with Toolumi are Google (ads on the Free plan, and the anonymous usage statistics described above) and Apple (purchases and the App Store), each under their own policies. Data on your device remains under your control and disappears when you delete it or the app.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws). Where a consent form applies you can withdraw your consent in the app at any time (Settings → Ad privacy options), which also stops the usage statistics. The statistics are not tied to your name, email address or Apple Account, so we cannot look up an individual’s data; deleting the app discards the app-instance identifier. Requests about advertising data should be directed to Google through the links in section 5. For anything else, write to the address below and we will respond.

10. Children

Toolumi is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested as non-personalized for all users.

11. Security

Your content stays on your iPhone and is protected by iOS. The few connections the SDKs make to Google and Apple use HTTPS. Password protection applied to PDFs in the app uses the system’s PDF encryption.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app’s behaviour changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top. The current version is always available at hamim44.github.io/privacy.html.
